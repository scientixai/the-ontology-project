EOP2 & analysis

The back bracket: the analysis traceability spine — native facts → ADaM datasets → Tables/Listings/Figures → endpoint results — and the End-of-Phase-2 decision node that rests on it: a coded go / no-go verdict, evidenced by the outputs, that yields a Phase 3 design. Every table is reproducible-to-source and grounded in the SAP; every result traces to its estimand.

The go / no-go isn’t a free-text meeting note — it’s a decision node: a coded verdict that rests on the analysis outputs and yields a Phase 3 design. A pivotal table with no SAP grounding is caught as post-hoc; a decision resting on nothing is caught as decision-by-assertion.

The EOP2 meeting stays a cr:RegulatoryInteraction (with its readiness warning), but the decision is now a first-class cr:AnalysisDecision: a coded outcome that cr:restsOn the analysis outputs and cr:yieldsDesign a Phase 3 design — so ‘what did the go-decision rest on’ is a graph walk. A cr:TableListingFigure must be reproducible-to-source (prov:wasDerivedFrom an ADaM dataset) and cr:prespecifiedIn the SAP — the analysis analog of the overstatement guard.

What it validates

ruleapplies toseverityrequires
cr:EndpointResultTraceShapecr:EndpointResultViolation
  • EndpointResult must reference the endpoint it measures.
  • EndpointResult must trace to a pre-specified estimand.
  • EndpointResult must trace to the SAP it was analyzed under.
cr:ADaMTraceabilityShapecr:AnalysisDatasetViolation
  • Analysis dataset (ADaM) must declare prov:wasDerivedFrom its source (traceability).
cr:TLFTraceabilityShapecr:TableListingFigureViolation
  • A TableListingFigure must be cr:prespecifiedIn a StatisticalAnalysisPlan. A table with no SAP grounding is post-hoc — inadmissible when presented as pivotal.
  • A TableListingFigure must declare prov:wasDerivedFrom an analysis dataset (reproducible to source).
  • TableListingFigure.tlfType must be 'table', 'listing', or 'figure'.
cr:AnalysisDecisionShapecr:AnalysisDecisionViolation
  • An AnalysisDecision must cr:restsOn at least one piece of analysis evidence (a TLF or an EndpointResult). A decision resting on nothing is a decision by assertion.
  • AnalysisDecision.decisionOutcome must be 'go', 'no-go', 'go-with-modification', or 'dose-selection'.
cr:EOP2ReadinessShapecr:RegulatoryInteractionWarning
  • EOP2 meeting lacks a binding agreement and/or a risk register (readiness warning).
cr:GoDecisionDesignShapecr:AnalysisDecisionViolation
  • A 'go' / 'go-with-modification' decision does not yield a Phase 3 design (cr:yieldsDesign). A go with no forward design is a decision without a consequence.
