# DTA reconciliation — three representations onto one authoritative model

Processes the CDISC DTA working-group handoff (2026-07-22). Reconciles the three
representations of the Data Transfer Agreement now in play and declares the model
of record. Every `.ttl` claim below is **verified against the raw file** at
`cr-domain/ontology/dta-module.ttl` (the handoff's model facts were `[Likely]`,
read from a summarizer, not raw triples — this document replaces that read).

## The three representations, and the decision

| Representation | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Discussion paper | Canonical DTA model; transfer archetypes vs business domains; provenance first-class | **Input** — reconcile its ideas into the model |
| `DTA_Template_v0.11` (Word) | MVP LB-focused template; USDM- and BC-bound; SDTM-LB variable list | **Projection** — generated *from* structured metadata (its own foreword says so) |
| `dta-module.ttl` | The TOP ontology module: 11 classes, PROV-native, bind-to-concept | **AUTHORITATIVE (model of record)** |

**Decision: `dta-module.ttl` is the single authoritative model.** This confirms a
decision the template designers already implied — the template foreword states it
is "the human-readable output generated by the DTA application from structured
study metadata," and that structured attributes "shall be considered the
authoritative source of information." Definitions authored as prose in the
template (the projection) will rot and drift. **Author in the model; project the
template.**

## The one correction the handoff couldn't make

The handoff was read from **`dta-module.ttl` alone**, so it concluded specimen
custody is simply missing. Against the **full `cr:` domain**, the finding sharpens:

- `hcls:Specimen` (in `hcls-core.ttl`) — the specimen entity **exists**.
- `cr:CustodyEvent` (in `cr-core-lims.ttl`) — a **full bitemporal chain-of-custody
  state machine** already exists: `SampleDue → Received → ToBeVerified → Verified →
  Published`, with current-state derivation proven by a detective test.
- `cr:onSpecimen` links results to `hcls:Specimen`. The DTA sodium worked example
  **already uses** `hcls:Specimen` + `cr:onSpecimen`.

**So the gap is not "no custody model." The gap is that `dta-module.ttl` does not
CONNECT to the one that exists.** Its five timestamps (`specimenCollectedAt`,
`specimenReceivedAt`, `analysisCompletedAt`, `reportGeneratedAt`,
`clinicalReviewAt`) ride loose on the result as datatype properties instead of
being a managed `hcls:Specimen` carrying a `cr:CustodyEvent` chain.

This makes the handoff's deepest framing **exactly right, and already true in TOP**:
"Sample Management has no link to Biomedical Concepts" because **custody is
provenance, not a clinical observation.** In `cr:`, custody is `top:Temporal` /
`cr:CustodyEvent` (the PROV spine); clinical content is `cr:BiomedicalConcept`.
The two axes are already separate — the DTA module just needs to ride the
provenance axis it currently only gestures at with loose timestamps.

## Execution plan

**Do now (clear, TOP-aligned, no external decision):**
1. **G1 — specimen custody reuse.** Connect the DTA transfer model to
   `hcls:Specimen` + `cr:CustodyEvent`; make the managed specimen explicit in the
   transfer context; keep the five timestamps as a *denormalized convenience view*
   over the custody chain (or derive them), explicitly on the provenance axis, not
   the BC axis. Add a shape (a transferred result must ride a specimen with a
   custody chain) + extend the sodium worked example. This is the handoff's #2 /
   highest-value gap.
2. **G2 — a binding pass.** For each Section-7 template variable, either bind
   (`boundConcept` + `codedAs` + `declaredUnitConcept`) or explicitly mark unbound;
   render into the template's empty CT/Origin columns from the model.

**Needs Bo's decision:**
3. **G3 — LUDWIG.** Verify what LUDWIG is; then place it as `codedAs` (if
   URI-resolvable) or `VendorAlias` (if a vendor dictionary).
4. **G4 — payload-shape archetype.** Adopt the partition or not (design-note Q6 is
   the current lean).

**Keep out of the model:** the prioritization decision record (criterion + order).
